Plan Guidance

Can I switch plans?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time through our member portal. Changes will take effect on your next billing cycle.

Is there a contract?

No. We believe in our service, so we don't lock you into long-term contracts. You can cancel at any time with 30 days notice.

Do you offer day passes?

We do! Day passes are available for $15, which can be credited towards your first month if you decide to join within 48 hours.
